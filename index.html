Baby,

Happy birthday to the person who has somehow made the most ordinary days feel like memories I want to keep forever.

When I think about us, I don't just think about the big moments. I think about all the tiny things. The way you notice the smallest details. The way you treat me like a princess without even making a big deal out of it. The random plans that somehow turn into the best days. The laughs that happen five minutes after a fight because apparently neither of us can stay serious for very long.

You are so much more special to me than I think I know how to put into words. I hope you always remember that there is someone in this world who notices you, chooses you, laughs with you, gets annoyed with you, and loves you through all the ridiculous little things that make you who you are.

And if I could choose one thing for you this year, it would be for you to see yourself the way I see you. Loved. Important. Worth celebrating. And deeply, deeply special.

I don't know what every year ahead of us will look like, but I know I want a lot more random dates, stupid jokes, MCD runs, memories, fights that last five minutes, and laughter that lasts much longer.

Happy 26th, baby. ♡

From the girl who will keep choosing you, again and again. ∞
